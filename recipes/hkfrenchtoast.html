Hong Kong-Style French Toast

One serving of this Hong Kong French toast makes a hearty breakfast for one person or can be shared as an afternoon snack for two. It’s perfect for those moments when you want something special without too much fuss.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices soft white bread
  • 2 teaspoons creamy peanut butter
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/3 vegetable oil or any neutral oil
  • 1 thin slice unsalted butter
  • Golden syrup or pancake syrup

Procedure

  1. Prepare bread and filling
  2. Create the Fully Egg Batter
  3. Heat the Oil properly
  4. Coat and Soak the Sandwich
  5. Fry to Golden Perfection
  6. Drain and Serve
